# Köşebaşı moodboard — üretim notları

Hazırlanan görsel: [Kosebasi-Moodboard.png](Kosebasi-Moodboard.png)

Amaç: Köşebaşı merkez yönetimine sunulan çatı hikâye önerisinin fotoğraf, ışık, renk ve doku yönünü göstermek.

Üretim yöntemi: Yerleşik imagegen aracı kullanıldı; CLI veya harici API betiği kullanılmadı. Altı kare tek moodboard olarak üretildi. Görsel, kaynak çıktısından değiştirilmeden bu klasöre kopyalandı.

Bu görseller yapay zekâ ile oluşturulmuş konsept referanslarıdır. Gerçek Köşebaşı şubesi, gerçek şube çalışanı veya onaylı menü fotoğrafı olarak sunulmaz. Gerçek çekimde ürünler ve sunumlar marka tarafından doğrulanır.

## Kullanılan son prompt

```text
Use case: ads-marketing.
Asset type: a single landscape photographic art-direction moodboard for a proposal to the central management of Köşebaşı, the Turkish restaurant brand founded in Levent in 1995, with roots in Adana and Tarsus cuisine. The proposal is ONE umbrella brand story, "Bir sofranın etrafında" (around one table), representing the whole brand rather than a campaign touring individual branches.
Primary request: create a refined, photorealistic six-frame moodboard demonstrating authentic culinary craft, fire, food texture and contemporary shared-table hospitality. This is conceptual imagery for choosing a future real production's visual direction, not documentary photographs of actual Köşebaşı locations.
Composition: landscape 3:2 board, preferably high resolution around 3000 pixels wide. A precise three-column, two-row grid of six equally sized editorial photographs, separated by thin warm ivory gutters and a narrow ivory outer margin. Each photograph should be a different scene. Fill the board with images, without a title band, typography, labels, numbers, logos, awards or watermarks.
Frame 1, upper left: intimate close-up of a Turkish grill cook's hands using a real curved double-handled zırh mincing knife on a clean wooden chopping surface. Show practiced, credible technique and the natural texture of ingredients. Focus on hands and craft; no gore or injury.
Frame 2, upper middle: authentic long Turkish Adana-style minced kebabs cooking on FLAT metal skewers above glowing charcoal in a professional grill. Restrained smoke, subtle embers, realistic meat texture and gentle backlight. Not Western steak or skewered meat cubes.
Frame 3, upper right: hero food photograph of a grilled Adana-style kebab with folded lavash, charred green pepper and tomato, parsley and sumac onion on a matte grey oval ceramic plate. Soft directional daylight, realistic juicy texture, contemporary elegant restaurant presentation with adequate portions.
Frame 4, lower left: overhead shared meal on a warm walnut table, with grey and ivory ceramics holding chopped tomato and walnut salad, herbed strained yoghurt, green leaves, lavash and kebab. Three natural adult hands reaching and passing a plate. A believable arrangement with room between plates and tactile folded linen napkins.
Frame 5, lower middle: a cropped, unposed moment of contemporary adult friends sharing a meal, photographed at table height. Natural hands, restrained everyday city clothing, intimate human gestures and passing plates, clear water glasses. Faces may be partially outside the composition; emphasis is on connection through the table, not models posing to camera.
Frame 6, lower right: editorial still life of tactile visual materials for the campaign: an ivory linen napkin, matte grey ceramic plate, warm wood, a little parsley and a softly blurred deep green background. A quiet composition communicating care and a modern urban Turkish restaurant, not a location overview.
Visual positioning: Turkish culinary heritage expressed through skilled hands and excellent ingredients, in a contemporary urban restaurant context. Quiet confidence, appetizing food and real human connection. Premium quality comes from light, textures, food styling and framing.
Color direction: warm ivory, muted forest and olive greens, charcoal, walnut brown, and natural paprika-red accents from food. These are a proposed photographic palette, not a new official brand identity.
Photography: sophisticated gastronomic editorial photography, authentic imperfect food textures, soft side light on plates, controlled backlight at the grill, balanced whites, subtle film grain, precise but natural color grading. Alternate close-up craft, food hero, overhead table and human detail.
Constraints: anatomically correct natural hands; realistic food, knife and skewers; no branch facades, addresses, city landmarks, store signage or recognizable named people. No alcohol, no Ottoman costumes, no ornate gold luxury set, no black-and-gold steakhouse aesthetic, no excessive flames, no orange color wash, no neon, no plastic CGI food. No lettering of any kind.
```
